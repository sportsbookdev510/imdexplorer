Hamster Nap (NAP), a last-buyer-before-bedtime game on Squeektoshi, SqueekChain's real hamster, on Ethereum Sepolia.

What it is: a hook launch on Sepolia (the standard token, its pool paired with Sepolia ETH, and one Uniswap v4 hook). Every buy of the token pays a small fee into a pot held by the hook and makes the buyer the latest buyer. When SqueekChain settles a nap of the hamster, the last buyer before the nap began takes the pot. A game lasts one running session: on 2026-10-30 the hamster took about three naps of 30 minutes or more a day.

SqueekChain facts (verify them on Sepolia before relying on them):

  • Live SqueekCore proxy: 0x980B4424c007CAf7a1f3670B48c8EaEf7EbC3d44, the core the SqueekChain API and livestream follow; it settles closed 600-second rounds about every 10 minutes. Do NOT use 0x74665dAecA35A2303e767E76a2FF1FB6F93e119f from the docs' deployment record: it has not settled since 2026-10-26.
  • configuration() reports genesisTimestamp 1793022600 and roundDuration 600; confirm the round numbering against the core.
  • state().settledThroughRound is the certified frontier; rounds after it are unknown, not zero.
  • roundPage(afterRound, throughRound, maxRecords) returns only rounds that had work (start exclusive, end inclusive, at most 16 records) and reverts on reversed ranges or ends past the frontier. A settled round missing from the page had zero work.
  • Docs: https://docs.squeekchain.com (the SqueekCore page and its published ABI).

Rules:

  1. A buy pays 2% of its Sepolia ETH into the pot; sells pay no game fee. Only a buy of at least 0.001 ETH makes the buyer the latest buyer; smaller buys still pay the fee.
  2. A nap is 3 consecutive settled rounds with zero work (30 minutes). It begins at its first zero-work round s.
  3. The winner of a nap that begins at round s is the last qualifying buyer whose buy happened before round s started. Buys during a nap or after it count for the next game.
  4. Anyone can call the claim for a settled nap. The pot collected from buys before round s goes to that nap's winner; fees from later buys stay for the next game. Each nap pays once, naps are claimed in order, and a winner that cannot receive ETH must not block the game.
  5. Failsafe: if the core's settledThroughRound has not advanced for 7 days, the last qualifying buyer can take the pot.
  6. No owner powers, no pause, no upgrade, no signer, no keeper, no withdrawal by anyone but winners. Sells always work, also when the core reverts. Constructors read no other contract: the core is read only at swap and claim time, and its address, the genesis and the round length are constants.
  7. Events for every qualifying buy, every claim and the failsafe.
  8. First game for Sworn card holders, on Ethereum mainnet only: when the hook runs on chain id 1, a buy during the first game (from the pool's first swap until the first nap is settled) needs balanceOf(buyer) >= 1 on the Sworn registry 0x0da2679000ea5380d1361cc3cca7d4e8c881f766, read live, so a wallet that mints a card later can join; after the first settled nap anyone can buy. On Sepolia (this launch) the check is skipped and anyone can play from the start. Sells always work. The buyer is the transaction's sender wallet (tx.origin).

Token and pool: the standard 1,000,000,000 token; all of the requester's ninety percent seeds the pool, nothing to a remainder wallet.

Tests and an independent review: Foundry tests for the round boundary (a buy in the last second before round s against the first second of s), naps of 2 against 3 rounds, a nap not yet fully settled, buys during a nap before it settles, several naps claimed in order, dust buys, the failsafe, ETH accounting (the pot equals fees in minus payouts), a winner contract that rejects ETH, and a reverting or paused core (sells still work). Use the live core's ABI and real round pages captured from Sepolia; a fork test at a recent Sepolia block is welcome. Then an independent adversarial review of the hook and its tests.

Website, after deployment: English; an unofficial community game with Sepolia test tokens of no value, not affiliated with SqueekChain. Show Squeektoshi live from https://api.squeekchain.com/v1/status (CORS open; poll every 10 s; running, resting, stale or offline), the pot, the latest qualifying buyer and how long ago they bought, the settled frontier and how many zero-work rounds a nap has so far, recent winners from the hook's events, a buy and sell panel on Sepolia with pending and error states, a Claim button when a settled nap is claimable, and a link to the console https://sepolia.squeekchain.com. Mobile first, a playful hamster bedtime theme.

Outside dependency: the live SqueekCore proxy above on Sepolia (read-only calls: state, configuration, roundPage). Fees and payouts: the 2% buy fee goes into the pot held by the hook, and the pot goes only to nap winners (or the failsafe winner); nothing goes to the owner, the requester or any other address.
