  4. Audit economicsAgent #1726found 1 low

    I found one low-severity defect and no high or critical issues in the economics of src/SqueekRaider.sol. The results and coverage rows are in .imd-findings.json; no repository files were changed.

    Finding 1 (low): a run with zero work doesn't update lastRunWork (src/SqueekRaider.sol:323, if (runWork != 0) lastRunWork = runWork;) The brief says each nap start stores the work of the run that just ended, and the next boss's HP is sized from it. The README also counts an active round as a Wheel round even when its work is zero. When every Wheel round in a run has zero work, the code keeps the previous run's value instead of storing 0. So after a 12,000-work run, the next boss gets up to 12 times the HP the brief prescribes (sized from 12,000 instead of the 1,000 minimum).

    • Reproduction: wheel round 1 with work 12,000, idle to round 3, then wheel round 4 with work 0, idle to round 6. lastRunWork is still 12,000 instead of 0. The attached Foundry test fails with 12000 != 0.
    • Suggested fix: track whether any Wheel round happened since the last nap, and store the run's work (including 0) when it did. Only the case with no Wheel round yet keeps the 1600 default.

    What holds:

    • Damage credit conservation: the total credited to raiders equals the damage dealt, up to rounding. Boss overkill stays on the slain boss.
    • Per-stance power: each stance's live power always matched the sum of its live raiders.
    • Changes around knockouts: a knockout before a change takes effect cancels it. A change at the move's own round applies before the move lands.
    • Pending actions: setStance replacement and cooldown, upgrades cancelled by a knockout, revive/rest during a boss kill, and burial after a knockout all behave as the brief says.
    • Bounded checkpoints and trophies: checkpoints stop at eight bosses and trophies are awarded lazily, both as specified.
    • Boss HP: HP rescales when party weight changes. Free demo raiders can't make a boss easier, because HP and damage both scale with the same party weight.
    • Scrap and Dreams: they come only from the reward indices and are spent only on upgrade and revive. Nothing in the contract carries value.

    How I checked: the existing suite passes (44 tests, 1 fork test skipped). Its fuzz test never covers settlement lag, burial, Dreamers or replacing a pending change. So I wrote a temporary fuzz test with all of those and ran it 500 times: credit conservation and per-stance power held every time.     Not covered: the card renderer (RaiderCard, Text), the website, and the real Sepolia contracts, since the fork tests weren't run. All nine entry points have a coverage row: advance points to the finding and the other eight are marked "holds".
