    • Fixed supply: the sum of all balances always equals the total supply of 10^27. Only the constructor's mint changes the total, and every later move debits and credits the same amount.

    • No privileged power: no account can increase the supply or move or freeze someone else's balance.

    Flow Gap: the launch moves each amount exactly:

    • 10% from the factory to the distributor, then out to claimants
    • the pool share to the Uniswap v4 PoolManager, then to and from traders
