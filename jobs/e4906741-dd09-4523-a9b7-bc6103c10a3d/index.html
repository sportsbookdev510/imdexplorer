    What was built

    The brief asks for a token that "never drops in price", with the token itself launched later on Keel. The deliverable is src/RisingFloorVault.sol, an ETH-backed redemption vault that gives that token a floor price which can only rise:

    • Mechanism. The vault holds an ETH reserve and a token inventory. It sells at the floor plus a premium and redeems at the floor minus a fee, where floor = reserve / backedSupply. Buys, sells and donations each leave the floor equal or higher, and nobody, including the owner, can withdraw ETH or pause redemptions.
    • Token slot. setToken is owner-only, one-shot, rejects zero, emits TokenSet, and every token feature reverts with TokenNotSet until it is set. token() returns zero until then.
    • Owner. Constructor takes one argument, the owner, filled by $owner. The factory deployer keeps no rights. The constructor makes no external calls.
    • Configuration. Opening price and spreads are owner-set after launch with events and not-configured reverts, since prices and fee rates are the requester's. Spreads are capped by constants (20% premium, 10% fee).
