Ship a game, tool or stream companion for the hamster economy:

Games and mods powered by Squeekchain

Races, bets or survival games that settle on real rounds, like predicting the next block or racing the hamster's live distance, or mods for existing games that add hamster-powered gameplay.

AI on the wheel

Use AI to watch, predict and create with the hamster: beat-the-AI round predictions, artwork that paints itself from every run, or game characters that remember their players.

Stream companions and overlays

Overlays for your own stream and second-screen apps built on Squeekchain's live API.

Utilities for holders

Non-custodial tools for staking, merging and managing Squeeks.

Apps for Squeek NFT holders

Naming, showcases, holder-only games or trading between holders.

SqueekFi

Staking strategies, prediction markets or lending built on SQK and Squeeks.

Dynamic NFTs that evolve with the hamster

NFTs whose art or traits change with the hamster's distance, round results or time on the wheel.
