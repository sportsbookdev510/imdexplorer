Hamster Hour: a pari-mutuel betting game on a real hamster. Squeekchain (https://sepolia.squeekchain.com) runs a physical hamster wheel and settles a round every 10 minutes with the number of wheel passes. Hamster Hour lets people bet IMD on how far the hamster runs over the next 6 rounds (one hour). Results come only from the IMD oracle.

CONTRACTS (Ethereum mainnet, no owner, no upgradeability, no admin withdraw). TEAM = the wallet that requested this launch, immutable.

  1. HamsterHour

  • Windows are defined by Squeekchain round numbers: window k covers rounds 6k to 6k+5. Round duration 600 s from genesisTimestamp 1791847800; the contract derives the current round from block.timestamp. Betting on window k is open until round 6k starts.

  • Bets in IMD (0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7) via transferFrom, minimum 0.5 IMD.

  • Three pari-mutuel markets per window, each its own pool: a) OVER/UNDER: total passes above or below the line. Line = average total passes over the last 7 settled windows, fixed at window creation; bootstrap line 1000 until 7 windows exist.

    b) EXACT: bettor submits a uint256 guess of total passes. Closest wins 60% of the pool, second 30%, third 10%; ties split equally. The EXACT winner also receives the full jackpot balance (see below).

    c) SLEEP: yes/no, total passes == 0.

  • Settlement: after round 6k+5 ends, anyone may call settle(k) with a signed IMD oracle attestation for the question template "What is the total number of wheel passes recorded by Squeekchain for rounds {6k} through {6k+5} inclusive, as published at {sourceUrl}? Answer as a single uint256." The contract verifies it through OracleVerifier and stores the result. Lazy: windows with no bets are never settled.

  • Split of every window's total bets: 91% to winners pro rata, 5% to stakers, 3% to an oracle reserve that reimburses 0.5 IMD to whoever supplied the settlement attestation, 1% to TEAM.

  • Staking: once setStakingToken is set, anyone can stake and unstake that token here. The 5% share is distributed pro rata by stake-weight at each settlement (accumulator pattern, pull-based claimStaking). Before the token is set, the 5% accrues in a reserve that is released to stakers evenly over the 30 windows after the token is set.

  • Jackpot: the contract accepts any ERC-20 sent to it; the balance of the token set once via setJackpotToken is paid in full to the EXACT winner of each settled window. Empty jackpot pays nothing.

  • Edge cases: a market with bets on only one side is void and refunded; a window with no valid attestation within 48 hours after it ends is void and refunded; all claims pull-based via claim(k).

  • Configuration: setStakingToken and setJackpotToken are one-shot; sourceUrl and genesisTimestamp are updatable. All four callable only by TEAM, each with a 48-hour timelock and a public event. Nothing else is adjustable. No other TEAM powers.

  • Events for every bet, settlement, claim, void, stake, unstake, config change.

  1. OracleVerifier
  • Verifies IMD oracle attestations exactly as the IMD attester scheme requires: attester signature, question hash, validity window, uint256 answer. Rejects expired, mismatched or replayed attestations.

TESTS: pari-mutuel math for all three markets, EXACT ties, one-sided void, no-attestation void, split sums to 100%, oracle reimbursement, staking accumulator, pre-token reserve release over 30 windows, jackpot payout, attestation replay rejected, line over 7 windows, round-to-window math around genesis, timelocks.
