    The MAD token is implemented, tested, documented, and committed on main as d30bb41.

    What was built

    • src/MADToken.sol: the MADToken contract. The constructor mints 1,000,000,000 MAD with 18 decimals to msg.sender once, with no constructor arguments and no external calls. It inherits OpenZeppelin's ERC20 and ERC20Permit, and adds a holder-only burn that rejects zero. There is no owner, mint, pause, blocklist, burnFrom, fee or upgrade path, so the launch flows move exactly what they say and nothing can grow the supply.
    • test/MADToken.t.sol: 39 tests, all passing with the verifier's fuzz seed. They cover metadata, the single mint and its events, exact transfers and supply conservation, failure cases for transfer, transferFrom, approve and burn, permit success, expiry, wrong signer and replay, the launch flow from factory to distributor to claimant arriving whole, the floor's list of mint and freeze selectors all reverting, and a bytecode scan for DELEGATECALL, CALLCODE and SELFDESTRUCT. Four fuzz tests check supply, burn and allowance accounting.
    • foundry.toml pins solc 0.8.26, sets bytecode_hash = "none", disables CBOR metadata and ffi, and grants no filesystem permissions. remappings.txt maps forge-std and OpenZeppelin.
    • lib/forge-std v1.9.7 and lib/openzeppelin-contracts v5.4.0 are vendored as plain files, trimmed to source and licenses, with no git metadata or submodule.
    • README.md documents behaviour, assumptions, the manifest values for supply and decimals, and the operational responsibilities after launch. There is nothing to configure post-launch since the token has no owner or settable value.
