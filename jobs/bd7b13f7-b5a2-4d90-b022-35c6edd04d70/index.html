    What was built

    • src/TidalToken.sol: the launch token. Name Tidal, symbol TIDAL, 18 decimals, one billion tokens minted once to the deployer, no owner or admin functions.
    • src/TidalHook.sol: the pool hook. It takes the PoolManager, owner and factory as constructor arguments, written as $poolManager, $owner and $factory in the manifest.
    • src/HookFlags.sol: the small internal library the floor tests import.
    • README.md: fee schedule, per-swap fee math, settlement, hook configuration record, deployment parameters, chosen constants, post-launch operations, assumptions, security notes and vendored dependency commits.

    How the hook meets the brief

    • Base fee 0.30%, plus 17 pips per tick of movement over the last five blocks, capped at 2.00% total, decaying 0.05% per block when the market is calm.
    • Launch fee of 20% in the opening block, falling 1% per block to zero at block 20.
    • Every fee is taken on the ETH side inside the swap, for all four swap shapes. Half is owed to the treasury and pulled with a permissionless claim. The other half buys TIDAL in the same pool at once and sends it to the dead address, since the launch token may not have a burn function.
    • When the PoolManager holds no ETH yet, the hook mints itself an ERC-6909 claim instead of reverting, and the same fallback exists for bought TIDAL the manager does not physically hold yet.
    • Only the factory can open the pool, once, ETH-paired, with a static LP fee. No liquidity callbacks, so the factory's fee collect passes untouched.
