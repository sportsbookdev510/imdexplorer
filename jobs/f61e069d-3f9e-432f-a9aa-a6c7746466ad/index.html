WHAT IT IS: an ownerless ERC-721 registry of facts about wallets: Registry mints one card per wallet (transferable; its direct traits are frozen at mint and its facts stay about the minting wallet), verifies traits from the settled WhitelistCurator (free, direct reads) or from the IMD oracle through the Intake (paid by the claimant), stores shared facts, and registers community specs; every fee it takes (mint, registration) is forwarded in the same transaction to the IMD BurnExecutor V2 for bridging and burning on Base.

Composer composes the oracle questions and validates specs; Renderer draws the card and its metadata on-chain. Anyone may call every function; there is no owner, setter or upgrade path. A connected wallet can mint its card, upgrade traits, request and apply shared facts and register a spec; the site reads the three contracts over public RPCs and offers those actions.
