miyamints is an NFT launchpad on Ethereum (later Robinhood Chain 4663) built on ms2fun-contracts. MiyaRegistry (src/registry/MiyaRegistry.sol) is a slim, non-upgradeable registry of factories, collections and alignment vaults; it holds the treasury and the fee terms each collection snapshots at launch: a 1% protocol fee to the treasury, a collection-chosen alignment share (minimum 10%) sent to a Uniswap v4 alignment vault, the rest to the artist. Factories: ERC721 fixed-price drops, ERC721 auctions, ERC1155 editions (also used for music drops) with an optional dynamic pricing module. Vaults (src/vaults/uni/) buy the alignment token with collected ETH through zRouter and add full-range v4 liquidity; benefactors claim LP fees pro rata. MiyaPool (src/pools/) is an NFTX-v2-style zero-fee pool: deposit an NFT for 1e18 pool tokens, redeem TARGETED (pick the id) or RANDOM (commit-reveal, ERC721 only), fixed per pool at creation. The deployment is script/DeployMiyamints.s.sol (runbook DEPLOY.md, per-chain config script/config/). An internal audit, three external area audits and their fixes are already in: AUDIT-NOTES.md lists the findings with their status, including what was accepted or deferred and the trust the keeper model adds. A finding already listed counts only if its fix is wrong or incomplete.
