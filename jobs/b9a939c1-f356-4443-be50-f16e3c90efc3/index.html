SqueekMatch: a non-custodial, fee-free Squeek merge-partner finder and P2P trading app for Squeekchain on Sepolia.

Problem: merging needs two Squeeks of the same level; a merged Squeek earns more power than the two combined. Holders with a lone Squeek at a level ("single") cannot merge and lose that power.

CONTRACT (SqueekMatchEscrow, Sepolia, no fee, no owner, no proxy, verified on Etherscan, events for every action):

  • Ask: maker lists a wallet-held Squeek for SQK (Squeek stays in wallet with approval).
  • Bid: maker escrows SQK for any Squeek of a chosen level, optional "merge-ready" filter checked on-chain at fill.
  • Swap + difference: Squeek for Squeek, either side may add SQK.
  • create/fill/cancel for each, expiry (default 7d, max 30d), bid refunds on cancel/expiry. Level and eligibility read from the Squeek contract at fill. Reentrancy guard. Tests for all paths.

WEBSITE (static, no backend):

  1. My Singles: connect wallet or paste address; per level paired vs single Squeeks, cooldowns, deposit state, "lost power" counter in SQK/day.
  2. Chain Planner: fewest purchases that unlock the most merges (e.g. 3xL1+1xL2: buy one L1 -> L2 -> L3), with cost, power gained, cooldown time at hamster pace; one click turns a path into bids.
  3. Match Board: all holders' singles by level from on-chain events (browser eth_getLogs, IndexedDB cache), filters, fair-value tag per listing.
  4. Trade: create/fill/cancel asks, bids, swaps; my orders.
  5. Buy then Merge: after a fill, pick survivor, preview new power, sign official Squeek merge / Staking mergeDeposited.
  6. Market summary: floor ask, best bid per level, last fills.

DATA: api.squeekchain.com (/v1/accounts/{address}, /v1/squeeks/{id}, /v1/economy/state, /v1/measurement/stats, /v1/status, /v1/events) for display only; every transaction reads the contracts. Verify contract addresses against Squeekchain Deployments docs and API identities; refuse trading on mismatch.

SAFETY: Sepolia-only, eth_call dry-run before every tx, warn seller to claim unclaimed SQK first (it moves with the Squeek), explain deposited Squeeks must be withdrawn to trade, show cooldown/scheduled power change before buying, confirm merge survivor (burned Squeek is permanent), plain-language errors, testnet banner.

Docs: docs.squeekchain.com (API, Merging Squeeks, SqueekNFT, Build owner actions). Fan-made, unofficial.

    The SqueekMatchEscrow contract stage is complete and committed. All checks pass: forge build, 92 tests across 8 suites under the verifier's fuzz seed and a second seed, and forge fmt --check.

    What was built

    • src/SqueekMatchEscrow.sol: one ownerless, fee-free contract with asks, bids and swaps, each with create, fill and cancel. Asks keep the Squeek in the maker's wallet. Bids escrow SQK for any Squeek at an exact level, with an optional merge-ready filter. Swaps trade Squeek for Squeek, pinned to a token or any Squeek at a level, with an SQK difference payable by either side. Expiry defaults to 7 days with a 30-day cap. The maker may cancel at any time and anyone may cancel after expiry, with refunds always going to the maker. Every action emits an event, every function is reentrancy guarded, and effects precede token moves.
    • Level and eligibility are read at fill from SqueekMining through the NFT's mining() pointer. Merge-ready mirrors SqueekMining's own merge precondition: live position, below the maximum level, no pending power change, cooldown reached at SqueekCore's completed block height.
    • test/: mocks of the four Squeekchain contracts, three reentrancy attackers, success and failure tests for every path, SQK conservation checks, and five fuzz tests.
    • docs/abi/SqueekMatchEscrow.json, README.md, a reviewable deploy script with a tested deploy function, and vendored forge-std 1.17.0 and OpenZeppelin 5.4.0 as plain files.
